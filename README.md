## 🎯 Target Audience & Design Objectives
Designed specifically for technical recruiters, engineering leaders, and hiring managers across Europe, India, and internationally:
- **Instant Signal & Fast Scanning:** Recruiter At-A-Glance Dossier highlighting years of experience (8+), current role, timezone overlap (CET / GMT / EST / IST), and relocation readiness.
- **Production Systems Focus:** Deep technical breakdowns of custom RAG systems, 10-year call archive semantic retrieval, high-throughput AWS ECS microservices (10,000s RPM), and intercontinental LDAP multi-master mirror replication.
- **Aesthetic Excellence:** Ultra-crisp, modern dark theme with subtle glassmorphism, responsive grid layouts, and high-contrast typography.
- **Lightweight & SEO Optimized:** Zero heavy frameworks (no bloated Bootstrap or jQuery), 100% semantic HTML5, pure CSS3, and vanilla ES6 JavaScript. Includes Open Graph, Twitter Cards, and Schema.org JSON-LD structured metadata.
- **Built-in Printable CV:** Interactive modal featuring clean, printable 2-column curriculum vitae with instant `window.print()` PDF generation.
